## Tutorials | Tenets of Treatment

#### "Limb salvage" 
Limb salvage typically consists of these three components:
1. [Identify foot infection](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/FootInfection/).
2. Apply [surgical techniques to treat foot infection](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/FootInfection/FootInfectionTreatment).
3. [Identify peripheral artery disease (PAD)](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/PAD/).
4. Perform [revascularization](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/PAD/PADtreatment).
5. Ensure [wound healing / foot reconstruction](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/FootHealing/) to restore an intact epithelial surface.

#### Palliative wound care 
For patients who won't benefit from limb salvage effort (ex. Provide palliative wound care for patients who won't benefit from limb salvage efforts. We have defined palliative wound care as **wound care done without the expectation of wound healing**.

#### Leg amputation
In selected patients, perform [leg amputation](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/LegAmputation/) _if_ this would **improve** quality of life and/or function.
