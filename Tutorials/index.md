## Tutorials | Tenets of Treatment

**Limb salvage** typically consists of these three components:
1. Identify and treat [foot infection](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/FootInfection/).
2. Identify and treat [peripheral artery disease (PAD)](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/PAD/).
3. Restore an intact skin surface, i.e. [wound healing / foot reconstruction](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/FootHealing/).

For patients who won't benefit from limb salvage effort (ex. Provide palliative wound care for patients who won't benefit from limb salvage efforts.

In selected patients, perform [leg amputation](https://nealbarshes.github.io/Tutorials/LegAmputation/) _if_ this would **improve** quality of life and/or function.
