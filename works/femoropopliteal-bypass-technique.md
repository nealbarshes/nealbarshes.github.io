  <section class="work-detail__section">
    <h2>Summary</h2>
    <p>This chapter provides a step-by-step guide to femoropopliteal bypass using autogenous vein or prosthetic conduit. It reviews indications and patient selection, preoperative arterial and conduit assessment, operative preparation, vein harvesting, exposure of the femoral and popliteal arteries, graft tunneling, and construction of the proximal and distal anastomoses.</p>
    <p>Additional sections address creation of a distal vein patch when prosthetic conduit is used, intraoperative verification of graft patency, wound closure, postoperative care, and expected outcomes.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="work-detail__section">
    <h2>Contribution and significance</h2>
    <p>The chapter translates a complex open revascularization operation into a practical sequence of decisions and technical steps. Detailed illustrations clarify vessel exposure, graft orientation, tunneling, and anastomotic technique in ways that complement the written description.</p>
    <p>Its comparison of autogenous vein and prosthetic options emphasizes how conduit selection, careful handling, precise graft length, and confirmation of blood flow affect bypass durability and limb-salvage outcomes.</p>
  </section>
